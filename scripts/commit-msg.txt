fix: a retried claim must not take more than its batch limit

The queue test failed intermittently: an emergency stop cancelled 0 jobs
instead of 1, because the claim had taken BOTH jobs.

The cause was in claimDueJobs. The claim is a single atomic

    UPDATE "SendJob" ... WHERE j.id IN (SELECT id ... LIMIT n FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED)

and it was wrapped in dbRetry. The comment claimed that a retry "can never
double-claim: the statement only matches rows that are still QUEUED" - and
while a retry can never claim the SAME job twice, it can claim a DIFFERENT
one. A retry after a lost response therefore took a second job and broke
the batch limit.

That matters in production, not only in the test: the worker would process
more than its configured batch, and the "n concurrent workers claim disjoint
batches" property would not hold.

The claim is now attempted once. Losing a claim is harmless by design: the
job stays CLAIMED with a lease, and when the lease expires
reclaimAbandonedJobs() requeues it. The worker's tick already catches a
failed claim and tries again on the next poll, so nothing is lost.

Verified: the queue suite passes (7/7) and the emergency-stop test now
sees exactly one cancelled job.
