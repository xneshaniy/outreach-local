fix: give contended transactions room to finish

The quota test failed intermittently, and the cause was a real production
bug rather than test flakiness.

A quota reservation serialises on a row lock: the first transaction locks
the sender's daily-usage row and the others queue up behind it. With
Prisma's default interactive-transaction timeout of 5 seconds, the queued
transactions were killed by the timeout instead of being told the quota was
reached. A measurement of the real path:

    elapsed: 5950ms | fulfilled: 5 | quota errors: 3

The logic was correct - exactly five reservations went through and three
were rejected with QuotaExceededError - but the run took longer than the
timeout, so under load the later transactions failed with a timeout instead
of a quota error.

That matters in production: with more than one worker running, the sender
quota reservation is exactly this contended path, and a burst of sends
would have produced a burst of spurious failures.

The Prisma client now sets transactionOptions globally - maxWait 15s for
getting a connection from the pool, timeout 30s for the transaction itself -
so every interactive transaction in the application, not just this one, has
room to wait for a lock it is entitled to.

The integration suite also has to run against the DIRECT endpoint rather
than a transaction pooler: it opens many transactions at once on purpose,
and a pooler cannot serve them. TEST_DATABASE_URL is now read from the
gitignored local file and points at the direct endpoint with
connection_limit=20.
